An add-on for RimTalk: pawns know where they are and who their job is aimed at, lasting conditions and new quests get talked about, and director's notes set up a conversation.

- Location: tells the model what this place is; you can write your own
- Job targets tagged: (colonist), (prisoner), (animal)
- Lasting conditions and accepted quests talked about
- Director's notes //...// and a "Make speak" button
- (Integration) world description for RimTalk - Expand Literature

Each part can be switched off in the settings. Does not alter saves. Requires Harmony and RimTalk; 1.6 and 1.7.
