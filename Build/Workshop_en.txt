[h1]RimTalk - Stagecraft[/h1]

An add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3551203752]RimTalk[/url]: pawns know where they are and who the job in their hands is aimed at, conditions that last for days and newly taken quests get talked about, and director's notes let you set up a conversation.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Location[/b]: every prompt tells the model what this map is: your colony, another faction's settlement (and whether they are hostile), a quest site (and for which quest), a cave beneath (and where it is entered from), and the region and landmark on the world map. Pawns stop taking an enemy camp for home. Select a place on the world map and press "Location description" to write its description yourself.
[*][b]Who the job is aimed at[/b]: RimTalk gives the model "Tending to Mira" or "Delivering food to Bob" with nothing but a name, and when that pawn is not nearby the model has to guess who they are. Now the name is followed by (colonist), (prisoner), (guest), (enemy), (animal, dog) or (mechanoid).
[*][b]Lasting conditions[/b]: RimTalk gives a letter one chance to be talked about, in the two hours after it arrives. A cold snap, an eclipse or toxic fallout that lasts for days is brought up a few more times while it lasts, as often as you set; if nobody took the letter's own chance, it is made up for.
[*][b]Quests[/b]: the moment you accept a quest, an awake colonist talks over the plan, who goes and what to bring.
[*][b]After a raid[/b]: once a hostile faction's attackers are down or gone (mods' raids included), the model is told whether it was won or lost, how long it lasted and how fierce it was, and what pawns say about it is up to it. Nothing is said if there was no contact or peace was made. It counts as lost if someone was carried off, loot was taken, raiders left after wrecking a large part of the base, half of those present died (at least two), or we lost people while no raider fell. Raiders who were driven off or gave up count as beaten.
[*][b]Director's notes[/b]: in RimTalk's "talk to" window, write //Amy and Bob argue about dinner, Bob gives in//. Nobody says it aloud; the model writes the whole conversation from it, with the pawn you picked speaking first. Text without the slashes is still spoken as a line.
[*][b]"Make speak" button[/b]: guests, traders and prisoners, who you cannot give orders to, get a button to start talking to someone. Let the AI write it, or write their line or a director's note yourself.
[/list]

Every part can be turned off on its own in the mod settings.

[h1]Compatibility and integration[/h1]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3633249209]RimTalk - Expand Literature[/url] (integration): its prompts for books and journals say nothing of the setting, and in a medieval game it wrote a diary full of patrol robots and satellite stations. Write a few lines about your game's era in this mod's settings and they are added to the end of its prompts.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3590863552]Progression: Education[/url] (integration): for the teacher and students of a class, "attending class" is followed by the name you gave it, so the model knows what they are studying.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3612632140]RimTalk Event+[/url]: works alongside. It lists the current conditions in every prompt; this mod has pawns bring them up again while they last. The setting "List current conditions in the environment" does the same as Event+; it is off by default, so leave it off when you use Event+.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3641774579]RimTalk Context Upgrade[/url]: works alongside. It makes the activity text more detailed; this mod only adds who the target is.
[*]With RimTalk's context optimization turned off, RimTalk itself gives job targets their age, role and race, and this mod adds nothing.
[/list]

[h1]Installation[/h1]
Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3551203752]RimTalk[/url]. Load order: Harmony → RimTalk → this mod. Supports 1.6 and 1.7.
Pure logic: nothing in your saves is altered, and it can be added or removed mid-game.
